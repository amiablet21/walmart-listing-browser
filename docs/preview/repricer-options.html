What the API allows, and how fast

From Walmart's developer docs. None of this is tested with your account yet.

Possible

  • Put items on a strategy, move them to another, or take them off.
  • Set each item's minimum and maximum price (minimum at least $0.01).
  • Read each item's strategy, min, max, price now and repricer status.
  • See which items have a Walmart-funded incentive (released today).
  • Create a strategy or change its rule: match or beat, by $ or %, pause it, how it treats promotions. Up to 200 strategies.

Not fast — it is an upload, not a live dial

  • One upload every 10 minutes; up to 10,000 items in it (Walmart suggests 1,000).
  • Wait 5 minutes before checking that an upload took.
  • Prices then follow; Walmart's seller guide says allow up to 4 hours.
  • Reading every item back is throttled too, so a refresh takes a few minutes.

Not in the documentation

  • No way to list your strategies or delete one. The app would learn their names from your items.
  • No stated way to change only the min or max; the app would resend the item with its strategy.
  • What the repricer status values are.
