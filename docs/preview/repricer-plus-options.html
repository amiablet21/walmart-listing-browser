What each part is built from

So you know what's certain and what depends on Walmart.

Incentive price column

  • The "During Incentive" price from your Incentive page, for the same SKU. Always available.
  • A small flag when your repricer Max is above it, plus a filter chip for those rows.

Market scan panel

  • The same sellers list, rank and verdict the Market tab shows, from the last scan. Nothing new is read.
  • One extra line the Market tab can't say: whether your Min is what's stopping the repricer from reaching the buy box.

Funded badge and pop-up

  • Which items are funded, their status and current price: certain (already working, 11 items).
  • "Customer pays", "Walmart covers", and the dates: only if Walmart sends them. Its docs disagree on this, and I haven't seen your account's answer yet.
