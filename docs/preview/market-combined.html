What moves

  • Strategy, Min and Max become three editable columns on the Market table.
  • Import from Seller Center and the Send tray move here.
  • The Repricer tab is removed.

What's new because they're together

  • An "at min" tag when your price is sitting on your minimum.
  • An "At min price" filter: the listings where the repricer has run out of room.
  • In the panel, how far the buy box is under your min, and the min that would let the repricer match it.

What gives

  • Item ID leaves the table (it stays in the panel) to make room.
  • The Amazon column leaves the table too; Amazon stays in the panel.
  • The six strategy items that aren't on your Incentive list get rows here, so nothing on the strategy is hidden.
